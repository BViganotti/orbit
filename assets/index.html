Model notes

Two-dimensional coplanar model. Earth–Moon–Sun positions come from the documented circular ephemeris; the Moon capture uses full n-body coasting and finite burns. Ascent includes drag, Earth rotation, staging and a gravity-turn autopilot. See docs/physics.md.
